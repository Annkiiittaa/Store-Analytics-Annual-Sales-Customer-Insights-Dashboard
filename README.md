# Store-Analytics-Annual-Sales-Customer-Insights-Dashboard
n interactive sales analysis report designed to explore Vrinda Store's 2022 sales performance, customer behavior, order trends, product categories, and sales channels—with the goal of identifying key customer segments and strategies to increase sales in 2023.

# Short Description / Purpose

The Vrinda Store Annual Sales Report 2022 is an analytical Excel report developed to understand the store's sales performance and customer purchasing behavior. The report focuses on monthly sales, customer demographics, geographical performance, sales channels, order status, and product categories to generate actionable insights for improving sales.

# Tech Stack

The project was developed using the following tools and techniques:

📊 Microsoft Excel – Main tool used for data cleaning, processing, analysis, and report creation.
🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation – Used to clean and prepare the raw sales data for analysis.
📑 Pivot Tables – Used to summarize and analyze sales, orders, customers, channels, categories, and state.
📈 Data Visualization – Used charts and graphs to present sales trends and customer insights.
🔢 Excel Functions – Used for data processing, categorization, and calculations.
📁 File Format – .xlsx for the complete Excel analysis and report.
4. Data Source

Source: AI-generated synthetic retail sales dataset created using ChatGPT.

A retail sales dataset containing approximately 31,000 order records was generated with the help of ChatGPT and intentionally structured to simulate a real-world retail dataset.

The dataset includes information such as Order ID, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Age Group, Date, Month, Order Status, Sales Channel, SKU, Category, Quantity, Amount, Shipping State, and B2B status.

The raw data was then cleaned, processed, analyzed, and transformed into an annual sales report using Excel.

# Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

Vrinda Store wants to create an annual sales report for 2022 to understand its customers and identify opportunities to increase sales in 2023.

Key questions such as: Which month had the highest sales and orders? Who purchased more—men or women? Which states contribute the most sales? Which age group contributes the most? Which sales channel generates the most sales? What are the different order statuses? Which category sells the most? … are difficult to answer quickly from raw data.

• Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive analytical report that:

Enables analysis of 2022 sales and order performance.
Helps understand customer purchasing behavior.
Identifies high-performing states, channels, categories, and customer segments.
Compares monthly sales and order trends.
Provides actionable insights to help increase sales in 2023.

# Business Impact & Insights

The analysis highlights women as the major customer group (~65%), with Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh among the top-performing states (~35%). The 30–49 age group contributes around 50% of sales, while Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra together contribute approximately 80% of sales.

Based on these insights, Vrinda Store can focus its marketing efforts on women aged 30–49 in the top-performing states, using targeted ads, offers, and coupons across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra to drive higher sales in 2023.
